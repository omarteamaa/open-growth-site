FAQ

Questions about web design in Dubai.

How much does a website cost in Dubai?

It depends on scope. Template sites in Dubai can start under AED 5,000, while custom business websites from established agencies usually run from AED 15,000 to AED 50,000 or more. Our bilingual builds start at AED 18,000 for up to 8 pages, AED 35,000 for sites with CRM and lead tracking, and AED 70,000 for e-commerce and multi-market GCC sites.

Do I need a bilingual Arabic and English website?

If any of your customers prefer Arabic, yes. Arabic is the official language of the UAE and many Gulf buyers search in Arabic first. A properly built Arabic version, on its own web address, lets you appear in those searches and makes Arabic-speaking visitors more likely to enquire.

How long does it take to build a website in Dubai?

Most projects go live in 6 to 10 weeks, and smaller Launch sites in 4 to 6. We agree a fixed timeline in your proposal before we start.

Do you really write in Arabic, or translate?

Arabic copy is written for Arabic readers, not machine-translated, and a native speaker reviews it before launch. We also design the right-to-left layout on its own instead of mirroring the English one.

What if the site doesn't bring in more leads?

We agree target numbers in week 1 and report against them every month. If results lag, the report shows what we think is holding them back and what we will change next.

Will I be able to edit the site myself?

Yes. Every site comes with an easy-to-use CMS and a handover session, so your team can update pages without us.

Do you work with businesses outside Dubai?

Yes. We are based in Dubai and work remotely with businesses across the UAE and the wider GCC.

Can I pay in stages?

Yes. Payments are split into milestones, which we agree in your proposal before any work starts.
